Privacy

Last updated October 15, 2026

This is my personal résumé site. It has no accounts, no forms and no comments, and I don't sell or share visitor data for advertising. The site itself sets no cookies. The only tracking on it is Google Analytics, which I use to count visits and see which links people follow.

What Google Analytics collects

When you open a page, a script from Google loads and sends Google information about the visit:

  • the page you viewed, its title, and the page or site that sent you here
  • your browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language
  • your approximate location (city, region and country), which Google works out from your IP address
  • the time of the visit and how long you stayed
  • a random ID stored in a cookie, so that repeat visits from the same browser can be counted as one visitor

When you click a link that leaves this site, such as my LinkedIn profile, Google Analytics also records the address of that link.

Your browser sends your IP address to Google as part of every request. Google says that Google Analytics 4 doesn't log or store IP addresses. I can't check that myself.

Cookies

Google Analytics stores two cookies in your browser for this site:

  • _ga holds the random visitor ID.
  • _ga_1DG579DN36 keeps track of your current visit.

Both expire two years after your last visit, unless you delete them sooner. No other cookies are set.

Why I collect it

I use it to see how many people visit, where they come from, and whether they click through to my profiles. That tells me whether my applications and links are working. I don't use it to identify individual visitors, and I don't see names or contact details in Google Analytics.

Your choices

This site doesn't currently show a consent banner, and it doesn't respond to "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals. The ways to opt out are on your end:

  • Install Google's Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Use a content blocker or a browser with tracking protection. Most of them block Google Analytics.
  • Block or delete cookies for this site in your browser settings. Page views are still sent, but each visit then looks like a new visitor.
  • Turn off JavaScript. The site works without it.

Links from this site to LinkedIn and other sites don't tell those sites which page you came from.

If you'd like your Google Analytics data deleted, email me the value of your _ga cookie and I'll ask Google Analytics to delete the data tied to it. Without that value, I have no way to pick out your visits.
